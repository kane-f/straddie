# Technology & Wi-Fi

## Wi-Fi

!!! warning "No credentials in this repository"
    The Wi-Fi network name and password are never stored in this public
    repository. See [Before You Come](../before-you-come/index.md#wi-fi-internet)
    for how they're provided.

The house runs a **mesh Wi-Fi network** for good coverage throughout —
several units working together rather than one router. Please don't
unplug, move, or reset any of the network equipment; if one part goes
offline it can take the whole network down, and it's not something that
can always be fixed remotely during your stay.

## TV

The three TVs in the house (one in each living area, one in the main
bedroom) are all **Samsung Frame TVs**.

- Feel free to add your own streaming service. If you do, sign out again
  before you leave.
- You can cast video content from your phone to the TVs.
- ABC and SBS both have good libraries online if you're after something
  different.
- Being Frame TVs, they display **artwork** when not in use, then turn
  to a black screen a few minutes after the room lights are turned off.
- Want a black screen straight away instead of the artwork? Press and
  hold the **power** button on the remote for about 5 seconds.

## Sound

There are SONOS speakers through the house.

- The Spotify app can cast directly to the speakers.
- To group speakers together, install the **SONOS** app on your mobile device.

!!! note "Be a good neighbour"
    Please keep the volume at a reasonable level when using the SONOS speakers.

## Smart Lighting

The lamps all have smart globes (**TP-Link Tapo**), controllable two ways:

- **Tapo app** — change brightness and colour from your phone.
- **Voice control** — see [Alexa & Voice Control](#alexa-voice-control) below.

!!! tip "A lamp flashing when you switch it on?"
    That usually just means it's been factory-reset — a previous guest
    or a power blip can trigger it. No need to try and fix it yourself;
    just leave it and use a different lamp or the switch. The owners can
    reconfigure it later.

## Alexa & Voice Control

There's an Alexa device in every room. Besides voice control, they'll
show you the weather and time at a glance.

Alexa can control, house-wide:

- **Lighting** — the Tapo smart lamps
- **Air conditioning** — each AC has a Sensibo controller, so Alexa can
  turn it on/off and set the temperature (see below)

**Voice commands (lighting)**

> "Hey Alexa, turn on the lights"

> "Hey Alexa, turn off the lights"

> "Hey Alexa, turn on all the lights"

> "Hey Alexa, set the lights to warm white"

> "Hey Alexa, set the lights to green"

Each room page also has its own voice-command cheat sheet for that
room's specific AC and lighting, e.g. "Hey Alexa, turn on the Main
bedroom AC" — see [House Guide](../house/index.md).

## Air Conditioning (Sensibo)

Most bedrooms and the upstairs living area have air conditioning, each
fitted with a **Sensibo** controller that's what makes Alexa voice
control possible.

!!! warning "Flinders bedroom has no air conditioning"
    Worth knowing when choosing a room, especially over summer.

Alexa can turn a room's AC on or off and set the temperature — see the
individual room pages for the exact phrasing that works in each one.
