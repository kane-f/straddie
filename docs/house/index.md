# House Guide

The Lookout Beach House is a two-storey holiday home — everything you
saw in the Airbnb listing, set up and ready to use from the moment you
arrive. This guide covers the house room by room; use it alongside the
listing photos to get your bearings.

## House Layout

- **Upstairs:** master bedroom, kitchen, and dining room
- **Downstairs:** the other three bedrooms, downstairs living room,
  [bathroom](bathroom/index.md), and [laundry](laundry/index.md)

Both levels have their own balcony, great spots to relax and enjoy the
sun — see [Balcony](balcony/index.md).

## Location

- **~5 minute walk** to the beach, the pub, and the bowls club
- **~15 minute walk** to the shops
- **~45 minute walk** to the centre of Point Lookout, including the
  gorge, ice-cream places, and the surf club
