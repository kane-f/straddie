# Balcony

## Overview

The house has two balconies — one upstairs and one downstairs — both
great spots to relax and enjoy the sun. The upstairs balcony is the
owners' favourite: relaxing on the deck, admiring the view.

## Quick Start

!!! tip "Take it in"
    Grab a drink and enjoy the view from either balcony — the upstairs
    one is what the owners love most about this house.

## House Rules

!!! warning "No smoking"
    Please don't smoke in the house or on the decks/balcony.

## The BBQ

The gas BBQ is downstairs at the rear of the house, not on the balcony —
see [BBQ](../../appliances/bbq.md) for how to use it and what to do if
the gas bottle runs out.
