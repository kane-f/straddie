# Cylinder

## Overview

Named after **Cylinder Beach** — the most popular patrolled beach, ideal
for swimming and beginner surfers thanks to its safe conditions. See the
full entry in the
[Island Guide](../../../island/beaches-and-swimming/index.md#cylinder-beach).

!!! info "From the photos"
    Looks like a queen bed with a street-facing window, plus a built-in
    closet — worth confirming the exact bed size and whether there's an
    ensuite.

## Technology

- **Air conditioning:** yes, with a Sensibo controller — see
  [Technology & Wi-Fi](../../../technology/index.md#air-conditioning-sensibo).
