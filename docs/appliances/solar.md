# Solar & Energy

## What It Is

The house has a large solar system on the roof.

## Quick Start

!!! tip "Make the most of the sunshine"
    During daylight hours the house generates a lot of excess
    electricity — more than the house typically uses on its own. If it
    suits your plans, running things like the
    [washing machine](washing-machine.md), [dryer](dryer.md), or other
    higher-energy appliances during the day makes good use of that solar
    generation, rather than drawing from the grid later.

There's no need to change your routine around this — it's just a
handy thing to know if you're deciding when to put a load on.
