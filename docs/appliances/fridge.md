# Fridge

## What It Is

A Samsung French-door fridge.

## Where It Is

Kitchen.

## Quick Start

!!! tip "Find the hidden panel"
    Part of the front door is a hidden panel — open it to reveal the
    **Beverage Centre**. It dispenses cold filtered water, so you don't
    need to open the main fridge door just for a drink.

## Common Tasks

| Task | Steps |
| --- | --- |
| Get cold filtered water | Open the hidden panel on the fridge door to reach the Beverage Centre |
| Get ice | The built-in ice maker keeps ice on hand — same Beverage Centre panel |

## Tips

The ice maker restocks itself, so there's usually ice ready without
having to think about it.
