# Shopping & Essentials

A short list of everyday shops and services on the island — see
[Food & Drink](../food-and-drink/index.md#groceries-self-catering) for
groceries specifically.

| Shop | What It's For | Location | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Point Lookout News Agent | Bookshop — newspapers, magazines, books, stationery | 1 Mintee Street, Point Lookout | Right by The Prawn Shack; worth a browse for a holiday read |
| Dunwich Cellarbrations / Point Lookout Bottle Shop | Bottle shops | Under the respective pubs, Dunwich & Point Lookout | Beer, wine, spirits |
| Straddie Market Co. Produce & Pantry | Greengrocer / pantry goods | Point Lookout | |
| Foodworks Point Lookout (Bob's Shop) | Supermarket | 1–2 Endeavour St, Point Lookout _(address needs confirmation)_ | Groceries, meat, fruit, veg, Swap & Go gas bottles, ice, bait, tackle |
| Foodworks Dunwich | Supermarket | 5 Stradbroke Place, Dunwich | Also has an ATM, near the ferry terminal |
| Straddie Super Sports | Sporting goods store | 18 Bingle Rd, North Stradbroke Island | Fishing rods, reels, tackle, bait; also does bike rentals |
| Straddie Servo & Hardware | Service station + hardware store | North Stradbroke Island | Fuel and hardware supplies in one — may be the same business referred to elsewhere as "Point Lookout Roadhouse"; worth confirming |
| Linen Caravan | Homewares, swimwear, gifts | Top of Endeavour Street, Point Lookout, near Foodworks | Soft furnishings, pots, plants, homewares, swimwear, apparel, giftware |

!!! question "Needs confirmation"
    - Exact street numbers and opening hours for most of these haven't
      been confirmed — this list is a starting point, not a verified
      directory.
    - Whether "Straddie Servo & Hardware" is the same business as the
      "Point Lookout Roadhouse" mentioned in
      [Practical Info](../practical/index.md#getting-around-point-lookout)
      hasn't been confirmed — could be the same place under a different
      name, or two different fuel stops.
    - No dedicated stand-alone hardware store beyond the servo/hardware
      combo above was found — worth a first-hand check if that's not
      right.
