## Best Views

Most of these double as some of the best viewpoints on the island:

- Point Lookout Surf Life Saving Club — headland position
- Stradbroke Island Beach Hotel (The Pub) — sunset drinks overlooking Main Beach
- Little Ship Club — waterfront, sunset views
- North Stradbroke Island Golf Club — views over Brown Lake, Brisbane
  harbour, Moreton Bay, and the Glasshouse Mountains from the course and deck
- Straddie Brewing Co.

Point Lookout Bowls Club is the exception — a good spot for a drink,
just not one that's specifically about the view.
