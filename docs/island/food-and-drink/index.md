# Food & Drink

## Things To Know

- Shops on the island are limited compared to the mainland — plan ahead.
- Woolworths delivers daily to Straddie; book your delivery in advance.
- There's no EV charger on the island — see [Before You Come](../../before-you-come/index.md#important-house-information) if you need to charge an EV during your stay.

## Point Lookout

### Not To Be Missed

The owners' personal picks — the four places they'd point you to first:

| Place | Address | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Whale Tail Gelati & Coffee Bar | 19 Mooloomba Rd, Point Lookout | Best gelato on the island |
| Stradbroke Island Beach Hotel (The Pub) | 158 East Coast Road, Point Lookout | Classic pub meals, free courtesy bus, live music weekends |
| Six Beaches Fish Grill & Bar | Point Lookout | Seafood — one of our favourite places |
| Fins 'n' Fries (takeaway) | Meegeera Place, Point Lookout | Fish & chips, cooked in cholesterol-free cottonseed oil |

!!! question "Needs confirmation"

    5 minutes (see [House Guide → Location](../../house/index.md#location)).
    Exact walking times for Whale Tail, Six Beaches, and Fins 'n' Fries
    haven't been individually confirmed yet — don't assume they're far

    time you're there rather than guessing.

books, and stationery. Worth a browse for a holiday read.
