# Food & Drink

## Things To Know

- Shops on the island are limited compared to the mainland — plan ahead.
- Woolworths delivers daily to Straddie; book your delivery in advance.
- There's no EV charger on the island — see [Before You Come](../../before-you-come/index.md#important-house-information) if you need to charge an EV during your stay.

## Restaurants & Cafes

| Place | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| Blue Room Cafe | Coffee, breakfast, lunch, ocean views, near North Gorge Walk. Vegan/vegetarian/gluten-free friendly. Arrive early for a window seat to watch for whales. |
| Six Beaches Fish Grill & Bar | Seafood — chowder, garlic butter king prawns, Moreton Bay bugs, ocean views. |
| Stradbroke Island Beach Hotel (The Pub) | Classic pub meals (parmi, seafood platters). Free courtesy bus, live music on weekends. Sunset drinks at The Point Bar overlooking Main Beach. |
| Straddie Brewing Co. | Three-storey brewhouse in Dunwich, seven craft beers brewed with Straddie aquifer water. Pizzas, tapas, seafood. Brewery tours available. |
| Straddie Eats | Seafood, burgers, salads — family-friendly, weekly specials. |
| Oasis on Straddie | Mexican & woodfired pizza, family- and pet-friendly, garden area. |
| Fins 'n' Fries | Takeaway fish & chips behind Foodworks, cooked in cholesterol-free cottonseed oil. |
| Whale Tail Gelati & Coffee Bar | Gelato, sorbets, coffee, near North Gorge Walk. |
| Perry's Fish & Chips | 16 Bingle Road, Dunwich. |
| Little Ship Club | 1 Yabby St, Dunwich — waterfront, seafood, steaks, sunset views. |
