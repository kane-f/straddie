# Beaches & Swimming

## Things To Know

- Always swim between the flags at patrolled beaches like Cylinder.
- Check surf conditions at the Point Lookout Surf Lifesaving Club.
- Planning some 4WD beach driving? See [Things To Do](../things-to-do/index.md#4wd-beach-driving).

## The Beaches

### Cylinder Beach

The most popular **patrolled** beach — ideal for swimming and beginner
surfers thanks to its safe conditions. Accessible via a car park, or a
40-minute walk from the North Gorge Walk.

### Frenchmans Beach

A secluded, **unpatrolled** beach near Point Lookout, ideal for a peaceful
walk or relaxing on the shore. Swimming — including snorkelling — isn't
recommended here due to dangerous currents and strong tides.

### Main Beach

A 30km stretch, great for experienced surfers and swimmers, patrolled in
sections. Also a popular [4WD beach](../practical/index.md#four-wheel-drive-tracks).

### Flinders Beach

A long, **unpatrolled** beach near Amity Point — great for fishing,
swimming, and 4WD adventures. Less crowded, with a raw, natural vibe.

### Deadmans Beach

A 600m **unpatrolled** beach with stunning rockpools, between Frenchmans
and Deadmans. Good for a quieter swim, but exercise caution.

### Home Beach

A tranquil, **unpatrolled** beach close to Point Lookout's main area,
perfect for a relaxing dip — and a prime spot to watch the sunset.

!!! tip "Our recommendation"
    Watch the sunset from Home Beach — a short walk from Point Lookout's
    main area, with fewer crowds than the other beaches. Bring a blanket
    and arrive about 30 minutes before sunset. Check tide times first.

## Surfing Lessons

Cylinder Beach and Main Beach both have lessons for beginners and
intermediates through providers like Stradbroke Island Surf School.
Equipment provided; sessions typically 1–2 hours, year-round weather
permitting.

!!! tip "Our recommendation"
    Book lessons ahead, especially during school holidays, and go for a
    morning session for calmer conditions.
