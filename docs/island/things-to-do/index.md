# Things To Do

For beaches, swimming, and surfing, see [Beaches & Swimming](../beaches-and-swimming/index.md).

## Things To Know

!!! warning "Respect Quandamooka Culture"
    The Quandamooka people are the Traditional Owners of Minjerribah
    (North Stradbroke Island). Some sites — Myora Springs, Brown Lake, Blue
    Lake — hold cultural significance; stay on marked paths and tread lightly.

## Walks & Nature

**North Gorge Walk** (1.2km, 30–45 min) — a scenic boardwalk with
breathtaking ocean views. Spot humpback whales (May–November), dolphins,
manta rays, and sea turtles. Starts opposite the shops on Mooloomba Road.

!!! tip "Our recommendation"
    Visit at sunrise for golden-hour views and wildlife spotting near South
    Gorge Beach, or across from Blue Room Cafe.

**Whale Watching** (May–November) — one of Queensland's best land-based
whale-watching spots. Try the whale-watching platform, Norm's Seat, or
Whale Rock on the North Gorge Walk. Best in September/October. Bring
binoculars.

**Hike to Blue Lake** (5.2km return, 2–3 hours) — a moderate hike through
Blue Lake National Park to a freshwater lake known as Karboora to the
Quandamooka people. Trail starts near Dunwich. You can swim here. Wear
sturdy shoes, bring water, avoid the midday heat, and check trail
conditions with Queensland Parks before heading out.

**Drive to Brown Lake (Bummiera)** — a perched freshwater lake with
tea-coloured water, about 15km from Dunwich (2WD or 4WD). Good for a swim,
picnic, or kayaking. Arrive early for a calm atmosphere.

**Myora Springs** — near Dunwich, a sacred Quandamooka site with
crystal-clear springs flowing into Moreton Bay. Easy 500m walk. **Swimming
is not permitted**, to preserve the site.

## Wildlife

Kangaroos, wallabies, and koalas are often seen near Main Beach or along
the North Gorge Walk — koalas particularly north of Amity Point, near
Ballot Street.

**Goannas & snakes:** lace monitors (goannas) are harmless but curious.
Carpet pythons (non-venomous) and the occasional eastern brown snake
(venomous) may be seen in warmer months. Keep your distance; if you
encounter a snake, stay calm and back away slowly — don't handle it.

!!! danger "Injured animal?"
    Call Wildlife Rescue Minjerribah: **0448 466 556**.

!!! tip "Our recommendation"
    Spot dolphins at Amity Point Jetty, especially early morning or late
    afternoon. Bring binoculars — combine it with fishing or a picnic.

## Water Activities

**Diving at Manta Lodge & Scuba Centre** ([mantalodge.com.au](https://www.mantalodge.com.au/),
(07) 3409 8888 — [book a dive](https://mantalodge.rezdy.com/218431/dive-stay-play)) —
beachfront at Point Lookout, trips into Moreton Bay Marine Park including
Manta Bommie (a top-10 Australian dive site). Manta rays and leopard
sharks (October–March), grey nurse sharks (April–September), turtles.
For certified divers; courses and equipment hire available. Book
ahead — morning trips usually have better visibility.

**Fishing** — Flinders Beach, Main Beach, and Amity Point Jetty are prime
spots. Target species: tailor, bream, flathead. Best early morning or
dusk; check tide times.

## 4WD & Beach Driving

A **Vehicle Access Permit** is required for beach driving — get one
[online](https://minjerribahcamping.com.au/4wd-permits/), or at
Minjerribah Camping in Dunwich ([minjerribahcamping.com.au](https://minjerribahcamping.com.au/),
(07) 3059 4042) — see
[Practical Info → Four-Wheel-Drive Tracks](../practical/index.md#four-wheel-drive-tracks)
for tracks and tide rules.

## Arts, Culture & History

**Straddie History Museum** (North Stradbroke Island Historical Museum,
Dunwich — [stradbrokemuseum.com.au](https://www.stradbrokemuseum.com.au/),
(07) 3409 9699) — exhibits on the Quandamooka people, early European
settlement, and the island's maritime and mining history.

**QUAMPI — Quandamooka Arts and Cultural Centre** (Dunwich —
[quampi.com.au](https://quampi.com.au/), 1300 004 183) — art exhibitions,
storytelling, and cultural workshops. Worth booking a workshop or tour
in advance by phone, especially for groups — no online booking found.

**Salt Water Murris Quandamooka Art Gallery** (Dunwich —
[saltwatermurris.com](https://saltwatermurris.com/), +61 484 501 268) —
Indigenous art gallery: paintings, ceramics, textiles, prints.

**Delvene Cockatoo-Collins Studio** (7 Stradbroke Place, Dunwich —
[cockatoocollins.com](https://cockatoocollins.com/), +61 476 791 692) —
art studio and retail store by Quandamooka artist Delvene
Cockatoo-Collins; handprinted textiles, ceramics, sculptures. Notable
works include the 2018 Commonwealth Games medal designs. Workshops
available.

**Starfish Studio** (Point Lookout — [starfishstudio.com.au](https://starfishstudio.com.au/),
0429 852 701) and **Linen Caravan** (Point Lookout — see
[Shopping & Essentials](../shopping/index.md)) — homewares, textiles,
and gifts, good for souvenirs.

**Straddie Tees** (Point Lookout) — island-themed apparel. _Trading name
unconfirmed as of an August 2026 check — may now trade as "Straddie
T-Shirts" or under a different shop name; no website or phone found
under either name — worth confirming next visit._

## More Things To Do

- **Golf** — North Stradbroke Island Golf Club (see
  [Watering Holes](../watering-holes/index.md) for contact details), a
  scenic 9-hole course near Dunwich with Moreton Bay views. All skill
  levels. Book tee times ahead by phone — no online booking found; bring
  cash (card facilities may be limited).
- **Tennis** — public courts in Point Lookout, open to visitors and
  locals. Bring your own rackets and balls.
- **Barefoot Bowls** — Point Lookout Bowls Club, 75 Dickson Way, Point
  Lookout (see [Watering Holes](../watering-holes/index.md) for contact
  details). Call ahead, bring flat-soled shoes.
- **Geocaching** — GPS treasure hunting around Point Lookout, including
  the North Gorge Walk and Cylinder Beach. Download the Geocaching app and
  check for caches before heading out — mobile signal can be patchy.
- **Stargazing** — minimal light pollution makes this a great stargazing
  spot. Bring binoculars or a telescope to a dark area away from town
  lights on a clear night.
- **Afternoon drink on the Surf Club deck** — Point Lookout Surf
  Lifesaving Club, overlooking Main Beach (see
  [Watering Holes](../watering-holes/index.md) for contact details).
