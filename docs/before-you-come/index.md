## Important House Information

**House rules:**

- No parties or events
- Only registered guests are allowed to stay overnight
- No smoking in the house or on the balcony/decks
- Quiet time after 11pm
- No eating in bedrooms or on sofas
- Wash any dishes prior to checkout
- Any damages are paid for out of the security deposit

**Parking:** the double carport is reserved for **unit 2** — please don't
park under the carport. Park in the car space under the house, or on the street.

**BBQ gas bottle:** if it runs out, see [BBQ](../appliances/bbq.md).

**Rubbish:** Monday morning is bin collection — see
[Before You Leave → Bins](../before-you-leave/checkout.md#bins).
